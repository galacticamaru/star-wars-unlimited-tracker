## Leia
This deck is built using Leader: Leia Organa – Get to your Transports! (Command/Heroism) on Base: Echo Caverns (Cunning). The deck comprises of 50 cards from these three aspects. There are 28 Ground Units, 12 Space Units, and 10 Events.

Ground Units 

2x C-3PO – Oh Dear, Oh Dear, 2x Chewbacca – Rrruuuurrr, 3x Echo Coordinator, 2x General Rieekan – Stalwart Tactician, 2x Han Solo – Scruffy-Looking Nerf Herder, 3x Hoth Trooper, 2x Ion Cannon, 1x Luke Skywalker – Do You Read Me?, 2x R2-D2 – Known to Make Mistakes, 3x Rogue Squadron Speeder, 3x Tauntaun Mount, 3x Trench Defender.

Space Units 

2x Bright Hope – Narrow Escape, 3x Evacuation Escort, 3x GR-75 Medium Transport, 1x Millennium Falcon – Bucket of Bolts, 3x Rebellion Y-Wing.

Events

2x Go For The Legs, 2x I’ve Found Them, 2x Improvised Detonation, 2x I’ll Cover For You, 1x Recovery, 1x Watch This.
