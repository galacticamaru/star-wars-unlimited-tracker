A: Inline, on the row that failed

The failing variant row turns red, the number shakes back to its old value, and a one-line message with a Retry sits directly beneath it.

Today this is silent. All three sections roll back and console.error — nothing reaches the user (variant-collection-section.tsx:52-56, same shape in the trade and want sections). Your number just snaps back.

Trade-offs

  • Unambiguous — the error is attached to the exact control that failed. With 4 printings × 3 sections there are 12 steppers; a generic message can't say which one.
  • Retry is in reach of the finger that just acted.
  • Layout shift — the row grows to fit the message, nudging everything below it.
  • Several simultaneous failures (offline) would stack multiple red rows. Judge whether that reads as informative or alarming — hit Fail all.

B: One banner at the top of the drawer

Failures collapse into a single region under the card title. It names what failed and offers one Retry for everything outstanding.

Trade-offs

  • No layout shift in the rows — the list stays stable while you work.
  • Scales to multiple failures — offline produces one banner, not twelve red rows. Try Fail all.
  • Weaker attribution. The banner names the variants, but your eye is at the row and the message is at the top. On a phone the banner may be scrolled out of view entirely.
  • Retry-all is coarser than retry-this.
Scroll the drawer down before triggering a failure. On a long card the banner appears above the fold and you may never see it — the same class of problem the merged bottom bar was designed to avoid in sketch 002.

C: Global toast

A toast at the bottom of the viewport, outside the drawer. One mechanism that would serve every optimistic write in the app, not just this drawer.

Trade-offs

  • Reusable everywhere — the binder, the deck builder and the catalog all do optimistic writes. This is the only variant that gives them one answer.
  • Zero layout impact on the drawer.
  • Ephemeral. If you look away it's gone, and the reverted number is left with no explanation — which is where this started.
  • No component exists. There is no toast primitive in src/components/ui/ today; this variant adds one.
  • Weakest attribution of the three — it names the variant in text only.
Note the honest problem: a toast that disappears after 4 seconds leaves exactly the silent-revert situation this sketch exists to fix, just with a brief announcement first.
