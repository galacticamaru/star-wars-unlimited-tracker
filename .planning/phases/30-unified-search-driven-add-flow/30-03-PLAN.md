<objective>
Rework the Manage Binder page into a single search-driven add flow over the full catalog, and delete the two old add surfaces.

This is the phase's headline change (BINDER-10/11/12): replace the owned-only "Add Cards to Binder" grid and the sidebar "Add Manual Want" box with one lazy, catalog-backed search; results open the extended sheet (Plan 02) where owned variants go to the trade binder and any variant can become a want. The full-catalog + owned-cards fetch is deferred to the first keystroke so nothing loads on mount.

Purpose: Deliver the unified flow end-to-end on the page users actually see.
Output: A pure merge/filter module with tests, a reworked `page.tsx`, and the removal of `manual-wants-add-flow.tsx`.
</objective>
